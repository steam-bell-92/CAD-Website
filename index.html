ABOUT US

The CAD Club provides students with the opportunity to visualize design ideas using software like CATIA and
SolidWorks Members gain a deeper understanding of design through renderings and simulations to assess real-world
performance.
CAD is a key part of Digital Product Development (DPD) within Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) and integrates with
tools like Computer-Aided Engineering (CAE), Finite Element Analysis (FEA), and Computer-Aided Manufacturing (CAM). It
aids in photorealistic rendering, motion simulation, and product data management (PDM).
Engineers use CAD for precise modeling, utilizing history, features, parameterization, and constraints to analyze properties
like tensile strength, stress, and environmental impact.



PROJECTS

ONGOING PROJECTS

Bionic Arm

Our CAD project focused on developing a functional, lightweight bionic arm prototype.
This model integrates precise mechanics with an ergonomic, custom-fit design for improved user control.
The core challenge was modeling the multi-degree-of-freedom gripper mechanism
and ensuring seamless structural integrity for 3D printing. The final design prioritizes modularity and uses advanced CAD analysis to optimize
weight distribution, demonstrating a comprehensive approach to medical device engineering.


Driveline

Our CAD work focused on modeling a robust and efficient driveline system,
the critical component that transfers power from the engine to the wheels in a vehicle.
Key models included the transmission shaft, the propeller shaft
with its universal joints, and the differential gear assembly.
The project prioritized designing components for optimal torque handling and minimal power loss,
showcasing precise tolerancing and stress analysis within the CAD environment.


Robotic Arm

Our CAD project involved designing a versatile robotic arm prototype,
capable of performing complex tasks with precision and repeatability.
The design process included modeling the base, joints, and end-effector,
ensuring smooth articulation and load-bearing capacity.
We utilized advanced CAD features to simulate motion paths and optimize the arm's kinematics,
resulting in a functional model ready for prototyping and testing.


Ferrari

Our CAD project focused on creating a detailed 3D model of a Ferrari sports car,
emphasizing aerodynamic efficiency and aesthetic design.
The modeling process involved precise surface modeling techniques to capture the car's sleek contours
and intricate details, including the chassis, body panels, and interior components.
We conducted virtual simulations to analyze airflow and optimize performance,
resulting in a visually stunning and technically accurate representation of the iconic vehicle.


Multi Terrain Vehicle

Our CAD project involved designing a robust multi-terrain vehicle,
capable of navigating diverse environments with ease.
The design process included modeling the chassis, suspension system, and drivetrain,
ensuring durability and performance across various terrains.
We utilized advanced CAD features to simulate real-world conditions and optimize the vehicle's dynamics,
resulting in a functional model ready for prototyping and testing.


RC Jet

Our CAD project focused on creating a detailed 3D model of an RC jet,
emphasizing aerodynamic efficiency and realistic design.
The modeling process involved precise surface modeling techniques to capture the jet's sleek contours
and intricate details, including the fuselage, wings, and engine components.
We conducted virtual simulations to analyze airflow and optimize performance,
resulting in a visually stunning and technically accurate representation of the aircraft.

Our members have achieved

CSWA : It is an entry-level certification for students and professionals seeking to demonstrate proficiency in CAD design. The CSWA certification proves foundational CAD skills, enhancing job prospects in engineering, product design, and manufacturing. It helps employers identify candidates with verified SOLIDWORKS proficiency, giving certified individuals a competitive edge.

CSWP : The CSWP (Certified SOLIDWORKS Professional) Certifications demonstrates advanced proficiency in SOLIDWORKS, including complex part modeling, assemblies, and design validation. It is highly valued in the industry, boosting job prospects and credibility for mechanical and industrial designers.
